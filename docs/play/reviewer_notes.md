# Notes for the Google Play reviewer (App access › instructions)

No login or account is needed. Messages++ is a messaging app (default SMS handler) that also sends
personalised messages to groups.

1. Open the app and follow setup: make it the **default messaging app** (Android asks), then allow
   Notifications, Alarms & reminders, Battery and Contacts.
2. **Messages tab:** your existing conversations. Send yourself a text from another phone: a notification
   appears with Reply and Mark as read. Tap **New message** to write to anyone.
3. **Contacts tab:** tap **Use phone contacts** to see the phone's contacts. Long-press one › Add to group ›
   New group "Test".
4. **Campaigns tab › New campaign:** name, Send by "Text (SMS)", message "Hello {first_name}", choose the group
   "Test", Save and review, then **Send**. The message appears in that person's conversation.
5. **Scheduling:** on the campaign, Schedule… › Repeat weekly, choose today and a time 2–3 minutes ahead, Save.
   Close the app; at that time it sends and a notification shows the result.
6. **Email (optional):** Settings › Email needs the reviewer's own Mailgun account or Gmail, so it is not
   required to review the app.
7. **Comeriver Pro** (Settings › Comeriver Pro) is optional: the free app is fully usable. Pro removes the
   "Sent via messages.comeriver.com" line from campaign messages and lifts the free limits (5 saved campaigns,
   1 schedule, 50 emails a day) and adds automatic birthday wishes. Personal messages never carry the line.

Standard SMS charges apply to messages sent from the test device's SIM.
