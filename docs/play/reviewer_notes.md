# Notes for the Google Play reviewer (App access › instructions)

No login or account is needed.

1. Open the app and follow the setup screens (Send SMS, Notifications, Alarms & reminders, Battery, Contacts).
2. Contacts › + Add: add a test contact with your own number. Groups › New group: add that contact.
3. New campaign: name, message "Hello {first_name}", select the group, Save and review, then Send.
4. To see scheduling: on the campaign, Schedule… › Repeat weekly, choose today and a time 2–3 minutes ahead,
   Save. Close the app; at that time the campaign sends and a notification shows the result.

Standard SMS charges apply to messages sent from the test device's SIM.
