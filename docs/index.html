Features

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Messages++ free?

Yes. Every feature works on the free app, with a few limits and a short signature on campaign messages. Comeriver Pro, a monthly or yearly subscription, removes them.

Who pays for the texts?

Texts go from your own SIM, so your carrier charges them like any SMS you send. We charge nothing per message. The app shows how many SMS parts a campaign uses before you send.

Why should I make it my default messaging app?

Android only lets the default messaging app send texts automatically. As the default, Messages++ shows all your conversations and sends campaigns by itself. You can switch back any time; your texts stay on the phone.

What if I don't make it the default?

Campaigns open your usual Messages app with everything filled in, so you tap Send. If everyone gets the same text, you can send to up to 20 people at once. Emails still go by themselves.

Does it work on iPhone?

An iPhone version is coming to the App Store. On iPhone, Apple requires you to tap Send for each text; emails go by themselves.

Can I message people who haven't agreed to it?

Please don't. Only message people who want to hear from you and follow your country's rules on marketing messages. Mark people as opted out and the app leaves them out of every campaign.

Does it send picture messages?

Not yet. Messages++ handles text messages; picture and group messages (MMS) are on the way.
