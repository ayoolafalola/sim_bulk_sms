Privacy Policy

Comeriver Messages · Effective 22 October 2026

Comeriver Messages (“the app”) is provided by [Comeriver – confirm legal company name] (“we”). The app lets you send text messages from your own phone and SIM to contacts you choose. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your contacts and messages stay on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.

Information the app uses

  • Contacts you add or import — names, phone numbers and optional email addresses that you type, import from a CSV file, or choose to import from your phone’s contacts.
  • Campaigns and messages — the messages you write, the groups you send to, schedules, and the status of each message (for example sent, delivered or failed).
  • Settings — such as the delay between messages, send limits and your default country.

All of this is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service, so none of this information is sent to us or to any third party.

Sending messages

When you send a campaign, the message text and the recipients’ phone numbers are passed to your phone’s SMS system (or, in some versions, to your Messages app) and delivered by your mobile carrier, the same as any text you send. Your carrier’s terms and charges apply. The app never reads, stores or uploads the SMS messages on your phone.

Permissions

  • Send SMS and phone state (Android, some versions) — to send your messages through your SIM and to let you choose which SIM to use. The app does not read your SMS or your phone number.
  • Contacts (optional) — only when you use “Import from phone”, to read names and phone numbers you select. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Notifications — to show sending progress, results and reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders and battery settings (Android) — to start your scheduled campaigns at the time you chose.

Your control and deletion

You can edit or delete contacts, groups and campaigns in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, permanently deletes all of its data. Because we never receive your data, we hold nothing about you to delete.

Your responsibilities

Only send messages to people who have agreed to receive them, and follow the laws on marketing messages in your country. The app helps by excluding contacts you mark as opted out or add to the suppression list.

Children

The app is intended for adults and organisations and is not directed at children under 13.
